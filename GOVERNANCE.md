# Governance

VirtualSensorMath is maintained by the VirtualSensorSoft organization. The
organization owner is the current maintainer and makes final decisions on
scope, compatibility, releases, and contribution acceptance. Additional
maintainers may be appointed through a reviewed repository change.

The project does not promise a release cadence or long-term ABI stability at
version 0.1.0. Changes to public behavior require tests, documentation, and a
reviewed pull request. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
