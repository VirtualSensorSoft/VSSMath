## Joe–Kuo D6 Sobol direction numbers

`src/probability/detail/SobolJoeKuoD6.inc` embeds the direction-number data
from `new-joe-kuo-6.21201`, provided by Frances Y. Kuo and Stephen Joe. The
table is the D6 set for dimensions 1–21201. The exact data bytes, excluding the
C++ raw-string delimiters, have SHA-256
`68eedd2a4e3b659b9695e7aff0f8ac68718bcf620730fc3d3a8c65df2a067441`.
Source: <https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~fkuo/sobol/>.
